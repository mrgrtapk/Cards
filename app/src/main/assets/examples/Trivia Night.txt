# Trivia Night — Jeopardy-style: the clue is on the front; answer in the form of a question.

Q: This planet's day is longer than its year: it takes about 243 Earth days to spin once, but only about 225 to orbit the Sun
A: What is Venus?
Venus also spins backward compared with most planets, so on Venus the Sun rises in the west.

Q: Napoleon's soldiers found this stone in 1799; its decree in three scripts helped Champollion decipher hieroglyphs
A: What is the Rosetta Stone?
The 196 B.C. decree appears in hieroglyphs, Demotic and ancient Greek. The British took it in 1801, and it has been in the British Museum since 1802.

Q: This element, symbol W, has the highest melting point of any metal
A: What is tungsten?
Tungsten melts at about 3,422 °C. Its symbol comes from its other name, wolfram, after the mineral wolframite.

Q: Completed in 1889 for a world's fair, this tower was meant to stand for only 20 years
A: What is the Eiffel Tower?
Built for the Exposition Universelle marking the centenary of the French Revolution, it survived partly because it proved useful for radio transmission.

Q: This composer was almost completely deaf when his Ninth Symphony premiered in 1824
A: Who is Ludwig van Beethoven?
At the Vienna premiere, a singer is said to have turned him around so he could see the applause he couldn't hear.

Q: The only letter that doesn't appear in the name of any U.S. state
A: What is Q?
Every other letter of the alphabet appears in at least one of the 50 state names.

Q: This mountain, not Everest, is the farthest point on Earth from the planet's center
A: What is Chimborazo?
Ecuador's Chimborazo sits near the equator, where Earth bulges outward, so its summit is about 2 km farther from the center than Everest's.

Q: In 1928 this Scottish scientist noticed mold killing bacteria on a culture plate he'd left out
A: Who is Alexander Fleming?
The mold was Penicillium notatum. Howard Florey and Ernst Chain later turned penicillin into a usable drug, and all three shared the 1945 Nobel Prize.

Q: This shortest war in recorded history, in 1896, lasted roughly 40 minutes
A: What is the Anglo-Zanzibar War?
British ships bombarded the Sultan's palace in Zanzibar after Khalid bin Barghash took power without British approval.

Q: Octopuses have three of these, and their blood is blue
A: What are hearts?
Two hearts pump blood through the gills and one through the rest of the body. The blood is blue because it carries oxygen with copper-based hemocyanin.

Q: This language has the most native speakers in the world
A: What is Mandarin Chinese?
English has more speakers in total once second-language speakers are counted, but Mandarin leads by native speakers.

Q: The Treaty of Westphalia, signed in 1648, ended this long European war
A: What is the Thirty Years' War?
The Peace of Westphalia is often called the start of the modern system of sovereign states.
