# Getting Started — a few cards about Cards itself. Delete this deck whenever you like.

Q: What's the difference between Study and Practice?
A: Study shows each card when it's due and spaces out your reviews so you remember them for longer.
Practice runs through cards any time, in any mix, and doesn't change your schedule.

Q: How do I study just one folder?
A: Open the folder and tap ▷ at the top — the Study & Practice menu.
It only uses the cards in that folder and the folders inside it.

Q: How do I rename, move, export, or delete a deck or folder?
A: Press and hold it.
To do several at once, use ⋮ › Select.

Q: What do the taps do in full-screen study?
A: Middle: show the answer.
Right side: next card.
Left side: previous card.

Q: How do I bring in cards I wrote on a computer?
A: Copy the files to your device, then open Import and tap Choose files.
Each file becomes a deck; folders become folders.

Q: How do I write a quick card on a computer?
A: One line per card, with two colons between the front and the back.
The Import page's “How to write cards” section shows every format with examples.

Q: How do I practice only the cards I find hard?
A: Star them, then open Starred and tap ▷ — or choose Starred in a folder's ▷ menu.

Q: How do I change the order of my decks?
A: Press and hold any folder or deck and choose Sort (or use ⋮ › Sort), then use the arrows — or tap Sort A–Z.
